Our story

We built it by hand.

Montecorboli is our home. For four years we've been getting it ready, one wall, one bed and one plant at a time.

I wanted somewhere for a certain kind of guest: people who like to walk or cycle all morning, read in the afternoon and eat well at night. It's ready enough to start now, and it will keep growing.

Everything here comes from the things I've always done for a living: painting, cooking and looking after people.

Come hungry, stay late, and leave with paint on your sleeve.

The studio

Paintings you can see, and take home.

My studio is part of the house. The paintings hang where we eat, and every one is for sale. Ask me about any piece you like.

I also host painting retreats and artist residencies. Artists stay at an agriturismo nearby, work in the studio during the day and come back here for dinner.

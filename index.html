how it started

We've spent four years building Montecorboli by hand: one wall, one bed and one plant at a time. Painting, cooking and looking after people are what I've always done for a living, and this is where they finally share a roof.

It's ready enough to start, and it will keep growing. If you like walking, books and a long dinner, I think you'll like it here.

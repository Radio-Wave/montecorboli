How it works

Simple, and all done for you.

  • Tell me the date, how many of you, and where you're staying.
  • We choose a menu together and I sort out any dietary needs.
  • I shop, cook, serve and clean up. You sit down.
  • Prices are on request and depend on the menu and group size.
